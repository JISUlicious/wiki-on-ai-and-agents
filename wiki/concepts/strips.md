# STRIPS

A planning system by Richard Fikes and Nils Nilsson, published at [IJCAI 1971](https://mlanthology.org/ijcai/1971/fikes1971ijcai-strips) and in *Artificial Intelligence* the same year. It represents actions through preconditions and effects.
