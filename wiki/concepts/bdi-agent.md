# BDI Agent

The Belief-Desire-Intention model describes a rational agent through what it believes, what it wants and what it has committed to do. It draws on Bratman's 1987 account of practical reasoning and was implemented in systems such as PRS. See [[bdi-agents-from-theory-to-practice-rao-1995]].

No checked LLM-agent paper cites it directly; see [[ai-agent-influence-graph]].
