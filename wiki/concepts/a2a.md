# Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A)

An open protocol announced by Google on 9 April 2025 for communication between agents across frameworks and vendors. Google positions it as complementary to [[mcp|MCP]], which connects agents to tools and context. See [[a2a-new-era-agent-interoperability-google-2025]] and [[acp]].
