# Announcing the Agent2Agent Protocol (A2A)

Google Developers Blog, **9 April 2025**. Introduces [[a2a|A2A]], an open protocol for agents to collaborate regardless of framework or vendor, launched with support from more than 50 technology partners.

## Key points

- Google states directly that A2A complements Anthropic's [[mcp|MCP]]: MCP gives agents tools and context, A2A connects agents to each other.
- It is released as open source, with a production-ready version promised later in 2025.
- The positioning comes from Google. Whether the two stay complementary in practice is a separate question.
