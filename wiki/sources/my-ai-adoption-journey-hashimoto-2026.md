# My AI Adoption Journey

**Mitchell Hashimoto**, personal blog, [5 Feb 2026](https://mitchellh.com/writing/my-ai-adoption-journey). A six-step account of adopting coding agents.

## Key points

- **Step 5 is "Engineer the Harness".** Hashimoto says he doesn't know of an accepted industry term and has "grown to calling this 'harness engineering.'" He defines it as engineering a fix whenever an agent makes a mistake, so that it never makes that mistake again.
- **He does not claim to coin it.** He says he'll adopt another term if one exists.
- **Two forms:** better implicit prompting through AGENTS.md, and actual programmed tools such as scripts that take screenshots or run filtered tests. Hooks and middleware are not named.

This is the earliest use of the exact phrase found while researching [[plugins-hooks-harness-influence-evidence]]. That is not proof of first use. See also [[harness-engineering-openai-2026]] and [[agentic-harness-engineering]].
