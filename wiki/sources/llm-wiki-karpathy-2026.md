# LLM Wiki (gist)

4 April 2026. Contrasts RAG, where chunks are retrieved per query, with an LLM that incrementally builds and maintains a persistent wiki. Karpathy relates it in spirit to Bush's Memex (1945). This wiki follows the pattern. See [[llm-wiki]].
