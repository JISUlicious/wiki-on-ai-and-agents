## Summary

**GPTs** are "custom versions of ChatGPT that combine instructions, extra knowledge, and any combination of skills."
- **No code needed:** anyone builds one in conversation by giving instructions and knowledge files and choosing capabilities (web search, image generation, data analysis).
- **Sharing:** privately, within a company (Enterprise), or publicly. A **GPT Store** with leaderboards and builder revenue was announced for later that month.
- **Background:** Custom Instructions (July 2023) came first. The post notes that "power users maintain a list of carefully crafted prompts", and GPTs package that practice.

## Actions: the successor to plugins

- "Like plugins, actions allow GPTs to integrate external data or interact with the real-world."
- "The design of actions builds upon insights from our plugins beta, granting developers greater control over the model and how their APIs are called."
- "Migrating from the plugins beta is easy with the ability to use your existing plugin manifest to define actions."
- Privacy: users choose whether data may be sent to a third-party API.

## On agents

"GPTs will continue to get more useful and smarter, and you'll eventually be able to let them take on real tasks in the real world. In the field of AI, these systems are often discussed as 'agents'." OpenAI frames its approach as moving "incrementally" toward agents.

## Why it matters for the wiki

- **The end of plugins:** GPTs and Actions replaced [[chatgpt-plugins]], which were wound down in March–April 2024 ([[chatgpt-plugins-wind-down-2024]]).
- **Packaged prompts, knowledge and tools:** the same instructions + knowledge + tools bundle reappears for coding agents as [[agent-skills]] (2025) and Claude Code plugins.
