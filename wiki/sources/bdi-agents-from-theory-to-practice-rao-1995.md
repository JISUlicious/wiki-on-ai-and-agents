# BDI Agents: From Theory to Practice (Rao & Georgeff, 1995)

Proceedings of the First International Conference on Multiagent Systems, 1995. The paper tries to connect formal theories of rational agents with implemented systems, using the [[bdi-agent|Belief-Desire-Intention]] model.

## Key points

- Theory and implementation had "proceeded in parallel" with little connection; bridging them is the paper's stated aim.
- It presents an abstract BDI architecture and its practical form, derived from the Procedural Reasoning System (PRS; Georgeff and Lansky 1986).
- The philosophical basis is Bratman (1987) on practical reasoning, with intentions as a response to resource-bounded deliberation.
- Motivating applications include air-traffic management, a real-time, high-stakes domain.
