# Atlas: Few-shot Learning with Retrieval Augmented Language Models

[arXiv:2208.03299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.03299), August 2022; the author list on the page has more names. Atlas tests whether a retrieval-augmented model, pre-trained for it, works in few-shot settings. Page 1 cites [[realm-guu-2020|REALM]], [[rag-lewis-2020|RAG]] and [[retro-borgeaud-2021|RETRO]] as prior retrieval-augmented work.
