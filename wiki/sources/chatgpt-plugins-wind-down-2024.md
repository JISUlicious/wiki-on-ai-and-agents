# Winding down the ChatGPT plugins beta

**Source**: `sources/chatgpt-plugins-wind-down-2024.md`
**Provenance:** OpenAI's help-center article could not be fetched directly. The quotes below are as reproduced in a GitHub discussion (danny-avila/LibreChat #1877) posted **2024-02-24**. Treat this as secondary.

## The dates

- "The creation of new conversations with plugins will be disabled on **March 19, 2024**."
- "Users will be able to continue existing plugin conversations until **April 9, 2024**."
- "On March 19, 2024, you will no longer be able to install new plugins or create new conversations with existing plugins."
- The notice pointed users to **GPTs** as the successor. It said the plugin store held a little over **1,000 plugins** during the beta.
