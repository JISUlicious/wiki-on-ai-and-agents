# Introducing Operator (OpenAI)

OpenAI, **23 January 2025**; read through a web.archive.org snapshot. A research preview of [[operator|Operator]], an agent that uses its own browser to do tasks, first for Pro users in the U.S.

## Key points

- It runs on the Computer-Using Agent (CUA) model, which combines GPT-4o vision with reasoning trained through reinforcement learning.
- OpenAI reports state-of-the-art results on WebArena and WebVoyager, its own claim.
- OpenAI planned to bring these capabilities into ChatGPT later.
