# Manus

A general-purpose AI agent from the startup Butterfly Effect, launched as an invite-only preview in March 2025. Secondary sources give 5 or 6 March; no official launch post was retrieved. [MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2025/03/11/1113133/manus-ai-review/) reports that it uses several models, including Claude 3.5 Sonnet and fine-tuned Qwen.

Its team's July 2025 post, [[context-engineering-lessons-manus-ji-2025]], is a primary account of [[context-engineering]] in practice.
