# Yann LeCun

French computer scientist, professor at NYU, and a shared winner of the 2018 Turing Award with [[yoshua-bengio|Yoshua Bengio]] and Geoffrey Hinton for foundational work on deep learning. He pioneered convolutional networks.

## Affiliations

- **NYU:** professor; listed as an NYU affiliation on the 2026 paper.
- **[[meta|Meta]] / [[fair|FAIR]]:** founded Facebook AI Research in 2013 and was later Meta's Chief AI Scientist. He left Meta in 2025.
- **AMI Labs (Advanced Machine Intelligence):** a Paris-based startup he co-founded on leaving Meta, with LeCun as Executive Chair. It builds world models on the [[jepa|JEPA]] approach. Press reports put its seed round at about $1.03B in March 2026, and differ on whether its launch counts as December 2025 or March 2026.

The Meta and AMI Labs details come from press coverage checked while ingesting the paper below, not from a source page in this wiki.

## Positions

LeCun is a long-standing critic of autoregressive next-token prediction as a path to human-level AI. He argues instead for [[world-model|world models]] that predict in an abstract embedding space. He proposed the [[jepa|Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture]] in 2022 as that alternative.

## In this wiki

- Co-author of [[platonic-representation-hypothesis-jepas-balestriero-2026]] (with Randall Balestriero), which proves the [[platonic-representation-hypothesis]] for JEPAs and argues that next-token prediction is an inefficient route to shared representations.
