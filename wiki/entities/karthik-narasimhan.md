# Karthik Narasimhan

NLP researcher and Princeton NLP principal investigator whose name runs through an unusually long stretch of this wiki's lineage: from [[gpt-1|GPT-1]] at [[openai|OpenAI]] (2018) to the agent-reasoning papers of 2022–23 and the software-engineering agent work of 2023–24. He was [[shunyu-yao]]'s PhD advisor at [[princeton-university|Princeton]].

## In this wiki

- **[[gpt-1-radford-2018|GPT-1]]** (2018) — second author with [[alec-radford]], Tim Salimans and [[ilya-sutskever]], at OpenAI. The paper that started the [[gpt-family]].
- **[[react-yao-2022|ReAct]]** (2022) — co-author; interleaved reasoning traces and actions. See [[react]].
- **[[tree-of-thoughts-yao-2023|Tree of Thoughts]]** (2023) — co-author as Yao's advisor. See [[tree-of-thoughts]].
- **[[reflexion-shinn-2023|Reflexion]]** (2023) — co-author (Princeton). See [[reflexion]].
- **[[swe-bench-jimenez-2023|SWE-bench]]** (2023) — senior author of the [[swe-bench]] benchmark.
- **[[swe-agent-yang-2024|SWE-agent]]** (2024) — co-author; the ingest calls him the Princeton NLP PI behind the [[agent-computer-interface]] work.

Read together, these trace one research line: from pre-training ([[gpt-1]]) to prompting LLMs to reason and act ([[react]], [[tree-of-thoughts]], [[reflexion]]) to evaluating and scaffolding them as software engineers ([[swe-bench]], [[swe-agent]]).
