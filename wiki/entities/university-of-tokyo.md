# University of Tokyo

Japanese university. In this wiki it appears on theory-leaning and architecture papers about how attention and transformer depth can be reformulated.

## In this wiki

- **[[expressive-power-looped-transformers-xu-2024|On Expressive Power of Looped Transformers]]** (ICML 2025) — Kevin Xu and his advisor Issei Sato, both at the University of Tokyo. It gives the first **approximation rate** for [[looped-transformer]]s, weight-tied blocks applied `r` times, and is a theoretical anchor for the looped / [[recurrent-depth-transformer|recurrent-depth]] cluster.
- **[[screening-is-enough-nakanishi-2026|Screening Is Enough]]** (2026) — Ken M. Nakanishi, listed with RIKEN (Center for Emergent Matter Science) and the University of Tokyo. It replaces softmax competition with bounded similarities and an explicit threshold ("screening"). The resulting Multiscreen model matches Transformer validation loss with about 30% fewer parameters.
