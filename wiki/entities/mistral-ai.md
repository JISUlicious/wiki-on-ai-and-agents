# Mistral AI

A French AI lab that releases open-weight models. Co-founder Arthur Mensch and Guillaume Lample are among the authors of its first paper. In this wiki it appears as the maker of [[mistral-7b|Mistral 7B]], which popularized [[sliding-window-attention]] with a rolling-buffer [[kv-cache]] alongside [[grouped-query-attention|GQA]], and of Mixtral (MoE). Mistral-7B is also a common evaluation model in KV-compression papers ([[kivi-2bit-kv-cache-quantization-liu-2024|KIVI]], [[snapkv-li-2024|SnapKV]]).
