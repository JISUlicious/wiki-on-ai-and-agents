# ChatGPT

[[openai|OpenAI]]'s consumer chat product, launched in November 2022. The 2022-11-30 date is general knowledge; no ingested source records it. It is built on [[instructgpt|InstructGPT]]-style SFT + [[rlhf|RLHF]] models.

ChatGPT is the product distinct from the models behind it ([[gpt-family]]). It is the point where LLMs reached a mass audience.

## Milestones recorded in the wiki

- **2023-03-23:** [[chatgpt-plugins|ChatGPT plugins]], with browsing (from [[webgpt|WebGPT]]), code interpreter, a retrieval plugin and third-party tools ([[chatgpt-plugins-openai-2023]]).
- **June 2023:** [[function-calling]] in the API.
- **Later:** Operator and computer use ([[computer-use-agent]]).
- **Classical framing:** the [[intelligent-agent-wikipedia|Wikipedia article on intelligent agents]] cites ChatGPT as an example of a goal-based agent.
