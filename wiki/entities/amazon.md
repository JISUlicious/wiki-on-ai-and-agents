# Amazon

Technology company whose cloud division, AWS, is the part that appears in this wiki. So far it shows up as an industrial affiliation on two papers, not as a model provider.

## In this wiki

- **[[sage-skill-library-wang-2025|SAGE]]** (2025) — **AWS Agentic AI** is the industrial home: most co-authors are there, and the first author interned there. SAGE folds skill generation into [[grpo|GRPO]] training so a [[self-improving-agent]] builds a [[skill-library]] across chains of tasks. It is evaluated on [[appworld|AppWorld]] with a [[qwen-2-5|Qwen2.5]]-32B base warm-started on [[claude-3-5-sonnet|Claude 3.5 Sonnet]] trajectories. Code is published under the `amazon-science` GitHub organization.
- **[[gated-sparse-attention-shen-2026|Gated Sparse Attention]]** (2026) — the paper has no affiliation line, so the only signal is the email domains. Alfred Shen has an `amazon.com` address and Aaron Shen a [[uc-berkeley]] one. The paper adds dual gating and adaptive sparsity to DeepSeek's lightning indexer ([[sparse-attention]]).

> [!note] Thin coverage
> Neither paper is an Amazon lab release. Add model-level work (e.g. Nova) or AWS inference infrastructure here if later sources cover them.
