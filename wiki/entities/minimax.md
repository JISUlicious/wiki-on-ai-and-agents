# MiniMax

Chinese AI lab that ships open-weight language models. This wiki holds its [[minimax-m2-series-minimax-2026|M2 series]] and its [[minimax-sparse-attention-lai-2026|sparse-attention]] work, and the name appears on some 30 other pages, mostly as a model in comparisons.

## Named in a distillation allegation (2026)

Anthropic's September 2026 [[anthropic-threat-intelligence-report-september-2026|threat report]] says MiniMax built a proxy network through an undisclosed shell company. According to the report, the service offered access only to Anthropic and OpenAI models and none of MiniMax's own, which Anthropic reads as evidence it existed to harvest user exchanges with US frontier models for training. This is Anthropic's allegation, and MiniMax's response is not recorded here. Details and caveats are on [[illicit-distillation]].
