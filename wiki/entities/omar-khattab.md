# Omar Khattab

Researcher at [[mit|MIT CSAIL]], best known for creating [[dspy|DSPy]], the framework for programming rather than prompting LLMs, and the ColBERT late-interaction retriever (Khattab and Zaharia, 2020; see [[dense-retrieval]]). Both began at [[stanford-university|Stanford]].

A recurring thread in his work is treating parts of an LLM system as artifacts that an LLM can optimize: prompts and programs (DSPy, [[gepa-agrawal-2025|GEPA]]), whole harnesses ([[meta-harness-lee-2026|Meta-Harness]]), and in 2026 the weights of a network ([[weight-space-programming]]).
