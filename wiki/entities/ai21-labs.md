# AI21 Labs

An Israeli AI company (Yoav Shoham and Shai Shalev-Shwartz are among the authors of the Jamba paper). In this wiki it is the maker of [[jamba|Jamba]], the "first production-grade Attention-SSM hybrid model". Jamba mixes attention and Mamba layers 1:7, cutting the [[kv-cache]] 8× against a Transformer with the same attention setup.
