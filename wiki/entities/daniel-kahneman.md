# Daniel Kahneman

Israeli-American psychologist. His Nobel-recognized work on **prospect theory** (with Amos Tversky) and on **heuristics and biases** underlies behavioural economics. The Nobel and Tversky details are general knowledge; the source credits prospect theory as "the basis for his Nobel prize".

His 2011 bestseller ***Thinking, Fast and Slow*** popularized the **System 1 / System 2** framing of [[dual-process-theory]]:
- System 1 is fast, instinctive and emotional;
- System 2 is slower, more deliberative and more logical.

The labels themselves were coined by Stanovich and West (2000). The book's reliance on studies later hit by the **replication crisis** has been heavily criticized.
