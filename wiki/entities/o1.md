# OpenAI o1

[[openai|OpenAI]]'s first "reasoning model" series (September 2024 in the wiki's timelines). Per its system card, the o1 series is trained with large-scale reinforcement learning to reason using [[chain-of-thought]] before it answers. The wiki treats it as the model that opened the [[large-reasoning-model]] era and the **comparison target** for the open models that followed. It has no source summary of its own, so everything here comes from how other pages use it.

## In this wiki

- **The bar DeepSeek-R1 was measured against.** [[deepseek-r1-2025|DeepSeek-R1]] reports [[aime|AIME]] 2024 79.8%, MATH-500 97.3% and a Codeforces 96.3rd percentile, "in the same class as OpenAI's o1 series". The comparison is implicit rather than head-to-head. [[deepseek-r1]] frames R1's contribution as showing that pure RL with [[grpo|GRPO]] and rule-based rewards elicits o1-class [[reasoning]] in the open.
- **A reference line on leaderboards.** [[gflowrl-scaling-distribution-matching-rl-liu-2026|GFlowRL]] quotes o1 at **1891 Codeforces Elo**, which its 14B model beats by +157, placing it within 25 Elo of o3-mini (2073). [[beyond-the-leaderboard-albayaydh-2026]] cites TravelPlanner going from 0.6% (GPT-4) to ~10% with o1-preview.
- **Heir of prompting-era test-time search.** [[self-consistency]], [[tree-of-thoughts]], [[chain-of-thought-prompting]], [[self-reflection]] and [[reflexion]] each list o1 as the point where their inference-time technique was internalized via RL. [[self-consistency-wang-2022]] calls self-consistency the conceptual ancestor of o1's thinking tokens.
- **Token recursion versus alternatives.** [[inference-time-compute]] classes o1 as *token-recursion* (more reasoning tokens) against *depth-recursion*. [[latent-thoughts-looped-transformers-saunshi-2025]] positions [[looped-transformer|looped models]] as a parameter-efficient analogue of o1 and R1, and [[latent-reasoning]] competes with the o1 lineage on that axis. [[sleep-time-compute-lin-2025|Sleep-time compute]] faults o1-style sequential test-time scaling for treating each query as stateless.
- **Retrieval mismatch.** [[when-to-retrieve-during-reasoning-guo-2026|ReaLM-Retrieve]] argues that LRMs such as DeepSeek-R1 and o1 need evidence injected during a long chain of thought, not before it.

> [!note] False friend
> **Search-o1** (Li et al. 2025), a baseline in [[search-r1]], is a retrieval-during-reasoning method, not an OpenAI model.

## Lineage

The first model in OpenAI's o-series. It departs from the [[gpt-4]] line by spending [[test-time-compute]] on a hidden chain of thought learned through RL, rather than scaling pre-training alone. The wiki mentions its successors only in passing: o3-mini, as a Codeforces reference point in GFlowRL. There is no o3 page, and [[reasoning-effort]] covers the knob that exposes this compute to users.
