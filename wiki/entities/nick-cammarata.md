# Nick Cammarata

Nick Cammarata is an interpretability researcher.

- **2020, at [[openai|OpenAI]].** Co-author of the Circuits thread's opening essay, [[zoom-in-circuits-olah-2020]], with [[chris-olah|Chris Olah]] and others. Its first claim was that features, often single neurons, are the readable units of networks, with InceptionV1 as the model. The same essay documented polysemantic neurons and [[superposition]].
- **2026, at [[goodfire|Goodfire]].** Author of "Neural Networks Are Already Clean" (2026-10-15). It applies the same neuron-level reading to the language model Qwen3-32B, reporting that about 90% of neuron *poles* have one main job. Those figures reach the wiki only through press coverage. The essay is the starting point of [[neuron-explorer]] and is contested (see [[monosemanticity]]).
