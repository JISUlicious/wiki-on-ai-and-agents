# Xiaomi

Chinese consumer-electronics company that also develops the **MiMo** family of language models, including MiMo-V2-Pro.

## Named in a distillation allegation (2026)

Anthropic's September 2026 [[anthropic-threat-intelligence-report-september-2026|threat report]] attributes a campaign to Xiaomi (GTG-16008). According to the report, Xiaomi saved its own users' conversations and coding sessions with MiMo models, many run through [[openclaw|OpenClaw]] and [[opencode|OpenCode]], and replayed them through Claude to produce SFT and RL training data. It counts over 400,000 requests across more than 1,500 proxy accounts in 20 days of March and April 2026. The report did not find that Xiaomi served Claude's answers to its users. It suggests MiMo-V2-Pro's extended free trial was used to attract developer traffic, noting the bulk of the traffic began as the trial ended. This is Anthropic's allegation, and Xiaomi's response is not recorded here. Details and caveats are on [[illicit-distillation]].
