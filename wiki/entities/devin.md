# Devin

An autonomous software-engineering agent from [[cognition|Cognition]], announced on 12 March 2024. Cognition reported 13.86% end-to-end on SWE-bench. It appeared about two months before the [[swe-agent]] paper's arXiv posting, though the SWE-agent repository already existed in April 2024. No citation between them was checked.
