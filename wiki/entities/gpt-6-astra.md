# GPT-6 Astra

A 2026 frontier model from [[openai|OpenAI]] in the GPT-6 generation. The wiki has no source page for its launch, so its release date, size and architecture are not recorded here.

## Lineage

It belongs to the [[gpt-family|GPT family]] after the GPT-5.x models; the wiki's sources mention GPT-5.6 Sol as an earlier OpenAI model, and a model ID `gpt-6-luna` also appears. What it inherited and changed is not documented in the wiki's sources, so no predecessor is set.

## In this wiki

- **Weight-Space Programming.** In [[weight-space-programming-takehi-2026]] it reads and writes neural-network weights directly, with no training. It named the games four policies play from their weights, fixed a stuck control policy, wrote MinAtar and Go players from zero weights, and removed a looping direction from Qwen3.5-0.8B (MATH-500 7.9% → 48.1%). It failed to identify a sycophantic Gemma model from weights alone. See [[weight-space-programming]].
- **Research-idea generation.** One of the generators compared in [[priced-guidance-future-research-ideas-wen-2026]].
- **ARC-AGI-3.** Its launch comparison reported GPT-5.6 Sol at 7.8% on ARC-AGI-3 and Astra itself at 99.9%, as recorded in [[harness-vs-model-attribution]]. Both are vendor-reported.
