# Lilian Weng

ML researcher and author of the **Lil'Log** blog (lilianweng.github.io), which is known for long, carefully referenced survey posts. In 2023 she was at [[openai|OpenAI]], where she worked on applied AI and safety systems (affiliation from general knowledge).

This wiki treats Lil'Log as a **trusted source**.

## In this wiki

- **[[llm-powered-autonomous-agents-weng-2023|LLM Powered Autonomous Agents]]** (2023-06-23): the canonical early survey of LLM agents. It frames the LLM as the agent's "brain" with three components, **planning, memory and tool use**, and names three challenges: finite context, long-horizon planning, and an unreliable natural-language interface.
