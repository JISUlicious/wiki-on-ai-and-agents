# University of British Columbia

Canadian university (UBC). In this wiki it matters as the academic home of [[jeff-clune]] and so of the open-endedness and self-improving-agent research his group produces.

## In this wiki

- **[[darwin-godel-machine-zhang-2025|Darwin Gödel Machine]]** (ICLR 2026) — the primary academic affiliation, alongside the Vector Institute and [[sakana-ai]]. Jenny Zhang, Shengran Hu and colleagues, with Clune as co-senior author, built a coding agent that rewrites its own scaffold and keeps an archive of variants. The [[darwin-godel-machine]] took a Claude-3.5-Sonnet base agent from 20.0% to 50.0% on [[swe-bench-verified|SWE-bench Verified]].
- **[[jeff-clune]]** — professor at UBC and Vector Institute faculty, with an advisory role at Sakana AI. His agenda is open-ended evolution, quality-diversity and AI-generating algorithms.
