# Ofir Press

NLP researcher at [[princeton-university|Princeton]] and a co-author on both halves of the Princeton software-engineering-agent program: the [[swe-bench]] benchmark and the [[swe-agent]] system built to attack it. Earlier, he was first author of ALiBi (Attention with Linear Biases), a [[positional-encoding]] scheme that lets a model trained on short sequences extrapolate to longer ones.

## In this wiki

- **[[swe-bench-jimenez-2023|SWE-bench]]** (ICLR 2024) — co-author, listed under Princeton. The benchmark frames real GitHub issues from 12 Python repositories as patch-generation tasks; at release the best model resolved under 2%.
- **[[swe-agent-yang-2024|SWE-agent]]** (NeurIPS 2024) — co-author. The ingest notes him as one of the principal voices of the SWE-bench / SWE-agent program, which introduced the [[agent-computer-interface]].

Both papers share a core team with [[carlos-jimenez]], [[karthik-narasimhan]] and [[shunyu-yao]].
